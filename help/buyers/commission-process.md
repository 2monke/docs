# How Commissions Work

Commissioning custom artwork from an artist on ArtPack is structured into clear phases: requesting, review & approval, payment, progress updates, and final delivery.

## 1. Submitting a Commission Request

When an artist has open slots, you can submit a commission request directly from their studio page:
- Choose the commission type or style that matches your project.
- Provide a clear description of your vision, character references, composition notes, and any deadline constraints.
- Attach visual reference files if needed.
- Review your details and submit. Your request will enter the artist's review queue.

## 2. Artist Review and Acceptance

After submission, the artist reviews your brief to determine if it fits their style and availability:
- If approved, the artist accepts the request and locks in the final quote.
- If the artist cannot take the project, they will reject or close the request, and you will receive an email notification.
- You are not charged when submitting a request; payment only happens after the artist accepts your project.

## 3. Payment and Escrow

Once your commission is accepted:
- You receive an email notification with a secure checkout link.
- Payments are processed securely via Stripe.
- Once paid, your commission officially enters the artist's active work queue.

## 4. Progress and Milestone Updates

While the artist works on your piece:
- You can monitor the progress directly from your Customer Dashboard under Your Commissions.
- The artist may share work-in-progress drafts, sketches, and milestone updates.
- You can communicate directly with the artist through the project comments thread.

## 5. Final Delivery

When the artwork is finished:
- The artist uploads the final high-resolution deliverables.
- You will receive an email letting you know your commission is completed.
- You can download your full-resolution files anytime from your Customer Dashboard.
- You will also receive an invitation to leave a verified review for the artist.
