# Payments, Currencies, and Refund Policy

All payments on ArtPack are processed through Stripe for bank-grade security and fraud protection.

## Supported Payment Methods and Currencies

- We support major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay, and Google Pay where available.
- Transactions are processed in the artist's configured studio currency (typically EUR or USD).
- If your card or bank account uses a different currency, your bank will automatically convert the charge at their daily exchange rate.

## Taxes and Invoicing

- Automatic sales tax or VAT is calculated during Stripe checkout based on your billing location and local digital sales tax regulations.
- Upon successful payment, a digital invoice receipt from Stripe is emailed directly to your billing email address.

## Refund Policy for Commissions

- Custom commissions represent time, effort, and bespoke artistic labor.
- Before work begins: If an artist has not yet begun drafting or working on your commission, you may reach out to request a cancellation and refund.
- After work has started: Refunds for active or completed commissions are granted at the discretion of the artist, subject to their individual studio terms.
- If you have an unresolved dispute with an artist regarding non-delivery, please contact ArtPack platform support.

## Refund Policy for Digital Downloads

- Because digital products (art packs, files, 3D assets) are fulfilled and unlocked immediately upon purchase, sales are generally final once the files have been downloaded.
- If a downloaded file is corrupted or technically defective, contact the artist or support for assistance.
