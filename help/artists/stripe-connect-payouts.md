# Connecting Stripe and Managing Payouts

To accept customer payments for commissions, digital products, and tips, you must link a verified Stripe account.

## Setting Up Stripe Connect

- Go to your Studio Settings in the Admin Panel and select the Payments tab.
- Click Connect with Stripe to begin Stripe's secure onboarding flow.
- You will be asked to provide your country, business or individual details, identification, and bank account for direct deposit.
- Once completed, Stripe verifies your details and redirects you back to your studio admin.

## Payment Processing Status

- Your studio dashboard displays your Stripe connection status:
  - Charges Enabled: You can accept payments from customers.
  - Payouts Enabled: Stripe can transfer accumulated earnings to your bank account.
  - Details Needed: Stripe requires additional verification documents before funds can be settled.

## Payout Schedule and Fees

- Payments are transferred to your connected account using destination charges.
- Payout schedules are determined by Stripe and your country (typically rolling 2-day to 7-day transfers).
- You can access your full Stripe Express dashboard anytime via the link in Studio Settings to view statements, tax summaries, and upcoming transfers.
