# Contacting Platform Support

If you encounter technical issues, account problems, or billing questions that cannot be resolved through the help center, our team is here to assist you.

## How to Reach Support

- Use the Help Widget on any studio page or dashboard and click Contact Support.
- You can reach out directly via email to our support team.
- For quick community assistance, join the ArtPack Discord server and ask in our support channels.

## When to Contact Support vs. The Artist

- Contact the Artist directly for: Questions about their specific style, commission timeline updates, revisions to active art pieces, or custom file format requests.
- Contact Platform Support for: Payment processing failures, login or authentication trouble, bug reports, account recovery, or unresolved transaction disputes.
