# Selling Digital Products and Bundles

Sell your brushes, layered PSDs, wallpaper packs, 3D assets, and guides directly from your shop.

## Creating a Product

- Go to Products in your Admin Panel and click Add New.
- Enter a title, description, cover artwork, and price.
- Assign a Product Type (e.g., Asset Pack, Wallpaper, 3D Model, Tutorial).
- Upload the downloadable files (ZIP archives, layered images, or documentation).
- Publish the product to make it immediately purchasable on your storefront.

## Product Bundles

- You can group multiple products together into a single discounted bundle.
- When creating a bundle, select the component products under the bundle settings.
- Customers who buy the bundle automatically gain access to all underlying component files in their customer library.

## File Upload Constraints

- Files uploaded for digital products are securely stored and delivered through authenticated download endpoints.
- Maximum per-file upload size is 30 MB. For larger multi-gigabyte collections, provide a structured multi-part archive or bundled download packages.
