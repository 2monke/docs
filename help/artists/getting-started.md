# Getting Started with Your Studio

Welcome to ArtPack! Your studio is your personal branded storefront for offering commissions, digital goods, and accepting tips.

## Setting Up Your Profile

- Log in to your Admin Panel and open Studio Settings.
- Set your studio display name, avatar, mascot image, and bio.
- Connect your social links (Twitter/X, Bluesky, Instagram, ArtStation, Discord).
- Customize your subheadings and welcome message for visitors.

## Choosing and Customizing Themes

- Navigate to Themes in your studio admin.
- ArtPack supports customizable visual themes ranging from minimalist editorial to vibrant aesthetic palettes.
- You can preview theme token changes live before publishing them to your public storefront.
- Configure brand accents, background tones, and card styles to match your personal art aesthetic.

## Custom Pages and Navigation

- Manage your storefront pages under Pages in the admin.
- Core system pages include Home, Prices & Commissions, Shop, and Queue.
- You can toggle page visibility, customize titles, and configure which pages appear in your header navigation.
