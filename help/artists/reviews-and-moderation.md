# Review Moderation and Featuring Testimonials

Customer reviews build trust and social proof for your studio.

## Viewing Received Reviews

- Open Commission Reviews or Product Reviews in your studio admin.
- View ratings, client comments, and submission dates.
- Verified badges indicate whether a review corresponds to an authentic, completed transaction.

## Featuring Testimonials

- You can feature outstanding testimonials directly on your home or commission pricing pages.
- Toggle the Featured switch on any approved review in the admin.
- Featured reviews automatically render in your storefront's testimonial showcase carousel.
