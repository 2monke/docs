# Digital Shop and Accessing Downloads

Artists can offer digital goods such as art packs, brushes, wallpapers, 3D models, and design assets through their studio shop.

## Purchasing Digital Goods

- Browse the artist's shop and select the products you wish to purchase.
- Add items to your bag or click direct checkout.
- Checkout is powered by Stripe with automatic calculation of applicable local sales taxes or VAT.
- Once your payment is confirmed, your digital files are instantly unlocked.

## Accessing Your Download Library

All digital purchases are permanently tied to your customer account:
- Go to your Customer Dashboard at `/dashboard/library`.
- You will find a complete list of every digital product and bundle you have purchased.
- Click the download button next to any product file to start your download immediately.
- If a product contains multiple files or is part of a bundle, each component file is available for separate download.

## Download Limits and File Expiry

- Your download links do not expire. You can return to your dashboard and re-download your files whenever you need them.
- If you made a purchase while not logged in, make sure to log in using the same email address entered during checkout to view your library.
