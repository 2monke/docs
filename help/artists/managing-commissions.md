# Managing Commissions and Sprints

ArtPack offers an end-to-end commission workflow designed to protect your time and keep clients informed without endless messaging.

## Opening Commission Sprints

- Under Sprints in your admin, you can create and open a new commission round.
- Set your slot limit (maximum active slots you are willing to take).
- Set customer request limits to prevent spam.
- Once opened, the commission form on your storefront becomes active for clients to submit briefs.

## Reviewing and Accepting Requests

- In Commissions, incoming requests arrive under Review or Needs Artist Review.
- Inspect the client's description, reference images, and requested delivery timeline.
- You can accept the request, adjust the final quote price, or reject the request with an optional note.
- When you accept, the client is automatically emailed an invoice checkout link.

## Working with Queue Milestones

- When the client completes payment, the project moves to your active Queue.
- You can set milestone checklists (e.g., Concept, Lineart, Flat Colors, Final Shading) to keep the client updated automatically.
- Clients can view live status updates directly on your public or authenticated Queue view.

## Finalizing Deliverables

- When the work is complete, upload the final high-resolution files under Deliverables.
- Mark the commission as Completed.
- The client receives an automated delivery email with download links and an invitation to leave a review.
