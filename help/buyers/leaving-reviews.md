# Leaving Reviews and Testimonials

Reviews help fellow art collectors discover trusted artists and provide valuable feedback to creators.

## Commission Reviews

- When an artist marks your commission as completed, you automatically receive a private, single-use review link by email (`/review/commission/[token]`).
- You can submit a star rating (1 to 5 stars), written feedback, and choose whether to post under your name or anonymously.
- Artists can feature top reviews directly on their storefront as testimonials.

## Digital Product Reviews

- If you purchased a product from an artist's digital shop, you can review it once the order has completed.
- Reviews submitted for shop items are automatically marked with a Verified Purchase badge.
- Each customer can submit one review per purchased product.
