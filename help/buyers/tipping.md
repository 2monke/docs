# Supporting Artists with Tips

You can support artists on ArtPack by sending a direct monetary tip along with a personal note of encouragement.

## How to Leave a Tip

- Navigate to the artist's studio page and select the Tip or Support option (or visit `/tip` on their studio domain).
- Select a preset amount or enter a custom tip amount (minimum amount is €1.00 or $1.00).
- Enter your email address to receive your payment confirmation receipt.
- Optionally, enter your name or handle, and write a supporter message.
- Complete the secure payment via Stripe.

## Where Tips Go

- Tips are routed directly to the artist's connected Stripe payout account via Stripe Connect destination charges.
- The artist receives an email and Discord notification with your supporter message and name.
