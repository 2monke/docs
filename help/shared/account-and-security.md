# Account Management and Security

ArtPack uses a secure dual-account model separating studio creators from buyers.

## Account Types

- Customer Account: Used by commissioners and art collectors to track active orders, download purchased digital files, and submit reviews.
- Artist Account: Used by creators to access the studio Admin Panel, configure storefront settings, and receive payouts.

## Password Resets and Security

- If you forget your password, navigate to the Login page and click Forgot Password.
- Enter your registered email address to receive a secure password reset link.
- Reset links are single-use and expire after a short validity window.
- Login forms are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile and rate limiting to safeguard accounts against unauthorized access.
